# Viva-safe 3-minute lesson flow

1. Open Lesson Builder.
2. Add title/source/topic.
3. Continue through Concepts.
4. Click Generate targets.
5. Hindi and Marathi can finish independently. Approve whichever is ready first; do not wait for the slower target.
6. Approve the remaining target.
7. Activities unlock.
8. Publish from Review. Publish does not wait for TTS.
9. For audio, click Generate only for the language you want to demonstrate. The job is one-shot and idempotent.
10. For the live translator, use the separate Live Translate page.

# Important demo rule
Do not generate a full 30-second Parler lesson audio live if the goal is a fast viva. On the tested RTX 2050, 30.2 seconds of audio took 116.12 seconds total generation time. Pre-generate a short sample when a spoken lesson asset is needed.
